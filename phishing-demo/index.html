Why Is Cyber Security Important?

🔐 Protect Personal Data

Protect passwords, photos, personal information and banking details from unauthorized access.

💻 Safe Online Activities

Students use the internet for education, social media, communication and online payments.

💰 Protect Money

Good cybersecurity practices can reduce the risk of online banking and payment fraud.

Common Cyber Security Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage systems, steal information or disrupt activities.

🔒 Ransomware

Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment from victims.

🔑 Password Attacks

Attackers attempt to discover or steal passwords to access accounts.

🌐 DDoS Attacks

Attackers overload a service with traffic, making it difficult for legitimate users to access.

👤 Social Engineering

Manipulating people into sharing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

How Can You Stay Safe?

✅ Use strong and unique passwords.

✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).

✅ Don't click suspicious links.

✅ Keep software and operating systems updated.

✅ Avoid entering sensitive information on unknown websites.

✅ Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.

✅ Keep important data backed up.

Cyber Security Careers

🛡️ SOC Analyst

Monitors systems and investigates suspicious security activities.

🧑‍💻 Ethical Hacker

Tests systems and networks to identify security weaknesses.

🐞 Bug Bounty Hunter

Finds security vulnerabilities in websites and applications.
